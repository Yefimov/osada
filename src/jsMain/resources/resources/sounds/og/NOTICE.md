# Open General SFX

These MP3 files are the per-equipment sound effects referenced by the Open General equipment data
used by OSADA. They were copied byte-for-byte from `SFX/` in the installed Open General
distribution; filenames and audio content were not modified.

Open General's `README/read_me_first.html` states that media files belong to their respective
designers and require the particular owners' written permission for redistribution. On 2026-09-01,
the OSADA repository owner confirmed holding or having obtained the required permission to
redistribute this SFX set with OSADA. Copyright in each audio file remains with its respective owner.

`manifest.json` lists only the referenced files present in that distribution. Referenced IDs whose
source files are absent are deliberately omitted and use OSADA's class-based fallback sound.
