What OSADA is

OSADA is a free, non-commercial, turn-based strategy game that runs in your web browser on desktop and mobile. You command army units on a hexagonal map in historical battles and campaigns, chiefly of the Second World War: you move, supply and upgrade your forces, fight for objectives and carry your veterans from one scenario to the next. OSADA is a community project based on the open-source OpenPanzer (Panzer Marshal) engine. There are no purchases, subscriptions or advertising.

Features

  • Single-player campaigns and standalone scenarios against a computer opponent.
  • Online multiplayer and cooperative games with friends.
  • Unit histories, heroes and campaign stories.
  • English and Russian interface.

No account needed to play

The whole game is playable without signing in. Saved games are kept in your own browser, and you can export them to a file at any time.

Why OSADA offers Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional and has one purpose: online saves, so the same campaign can be continued on another computer or phone. OSADA asks Google only for the openid permission and stores only the anonymous account identifier Google provides for OSADA, to know which saved games are yours. OSADA does not read or store your email address, name, photo, contacts or any other Google data, and does not use Google data for advertising, artificial intelligence, image generation or any purpose other than storing your saved games. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
